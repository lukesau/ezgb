Where the logic and wiring of the EZ Flash Jr's FPGA sit on the chip, for firmware FW4 and FW5 0918. EZ Flash designed these; this map is decoded from their bitstreams by the ezgb reverse-engineering project, which is not affiliated with EZ Flash. Block RAM contents aren't included. Select a block or any cell to explore.

Select a block to see it alone on both chips. cells counts its LUTs, flip-flops, wide muxes and carry bits. wire is the tiles of routing its outputs use.
